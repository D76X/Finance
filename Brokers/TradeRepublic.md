
# Are PAC available on all ETF on Trade Republic?

No, a PAC (Piano di Accumulo del Capitale, or savings plan) is not available on every 
single ETF on Trade Republic. 

[1] (https://extraetf.com/it/pac-etf/offerta-pac-etf-trade-republic)

- Available Selection: 
Trade Republic offers over 2,000 to 2,500 ETFs eligible for a PAC 
(many of which have €0 execution fees), out of the total tradeable instruments 
on the platform. 

[1] (https://www.finanzapp.io/blog-ita/pac-etf-250-euro-al-mese), 
[2] (https://www.finanzapp.io/blog-ita/migliori-etf-pac-italia-2026), 
[3] (https://extraetf.com/it/pac-etf/offerta-pac-etf-trade-republic)

- Not Universal: 
While a massive range of popular equity, bond, commodity, and real estate ETFs 
support automated savings plans, niche or newly listed ETFs might only be available 
for single-order trading or not supported for periodic PAC investments.

You can check specific ETF eligibility directly inside the app under the `Security/Investment` 
tab or via the justETF Trade Republic Directory. 

[1] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/trade-republic-piano-di-accumulo.html)

# Is there a limit to the number of time I can stop or change the amount of a PAC on Trade Republic?

No, there is no limit to the number of times you can stop, pause, or change the amount of a savings plan 
(PAC - Piano di Accumulo) on Trade Republic. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/760-Cosa-sono-i-piani-di-accumulo)

You retain full flexibility over your investments, allowing you to modify your strategy as your 
financial situation changes. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/760-What-are-savings-plans), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

## Key Rules & Flexibility Rules

- Completely free updates: 
Adjusting the amount, frequency, or asset involves zero fees. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/760-Cosa-sono-i-piani-di-accumulo)

- Unlimited cancellations: 
Delete and restart a plan whenever you want. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

- No lock-in periods: 
Your accumulated fractional shares remain safely in your custody even if you stop the plan. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/760-What-are-savings-plans), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo)

- Automatic insufficient fund handling: 
If your cash balance is empty on execution day, Trade Republic simply skips that month's deposit 
without penalizing or charging you. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/910-Cosa-succede-se-nel-conto-di-transito-non-ci-sono-fondi-sufficienti-per-eseguire-il-piano-di-investimento)

## How to Make Changes in the App

1. Open the app and navigate to Wealth (or Investments). 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/890-Quando-viene-eseguito-il-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/760-What-are-savings-plans)

2. Tap Orders & Insights and select your active Savings Plan. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/890-Quando-viene-eseguito-il-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

3. Select the specific asset plan you want to modify. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo)

4. Tap Change to adjust the execution amount/frequency, or tap Delete to stop it entirely. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

# How do pac work on trade republic?

[How to Set Up an AUTOMATIC FREE Savings Plan on TRADE REPUBLIC 📈 (ETFs, STOCKS, or CRYPTO) simodefa](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_F5sqiB_2hE)  

A PAC (Piano di Accumulo del Capitale, or savings plan) on Trade Republic 
allows you to automatically invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals 
into ETFs, stocks, or cryptocurrencies. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

## How It Works

- Choose the Asset: 
Select the ETF, stock, or crypto asset you want to invest in using the app 
(searching by ISIN is recommended to pick the exact instrument). 

[1] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/trade-republic-piano-di-accumulo.html), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

- Set the Amount & Frequency: 
Decide how much you want to invest (even small amounts are supported) and choose a 
frequency such as weekly, monthly, bi-monthly, or quarterly. 

[1] (https://www.finanzapp.io/blog-ita/pac-etf-250-euro-al-mese), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

- Execution Date & Funding: 
Pick your preferred execution date (e.g., start or middle of the month). 
The money is automatically debited from your Trade Republic cash account (or via direct debit if set up). 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/890-Quando-viene-eseguito-il-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio), 
[3] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/trade-republic-piano-di-accumulo.html)

- Fractional Shares: 
If your recurring investment amount does not equal the exact price of a full share, 
Trade Republic automatically purchases fractional shares for you. 

[1] (https://www.tiktok.com/@futurascf/video/7675305547196812566)

- Free Modification or Cancellation: 
You can pause, edit the amount/frequency, or cancel the savings plan for free at any time 
directly inside the app. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)
You can set up a plan via the Trade Republic Support Guide. 
[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)
