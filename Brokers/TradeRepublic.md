
# Can I transfer my ETFs from Trading 212 to Trade Republic?

Yes, you can transfer your ETFs from Trading 212 to Trade Republic. 

Both brokers support in-specie (stock-for-stock) portfolio transfers, allowing you to move 
your investments without selling them first. Neither Trading 212 nor Trade Republic charges 
any commission or fee to process this request. 

[1] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer), 
[2] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007081117-Can-I-move-my-Trading-212-ISA-to-another-provider), [3] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/16105401083805-What-is-a-portfolio-transfer), 
[4] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-sk/825-How-long-does-a-securities-account-transfer-from-Trade-Republic-to-a-third_party-custody-account-take)

However, some technical and regional conditions must be met before starting. 

For instance, fractional shares cannot be transferred directly and must be sold to cash beforehand. 

[1] (https://dividendmapper.com/blog/transfer-isa-to-trading-212), 
[2] (https://www.tiktok.com/discover/how-to-transfer-stocks-from-trading-212-to-trade-republic)

## Key Limitations & Requirements

- German resident restriction: 
Portfolio transfers are unavailable for Trading 212 EU GmbH users. 

[1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/16105401083805-What-is-a-portfolio-transfer)

- Whole shares only: 
Fractional holdings cannot be moved across brokers.
	- Sell fractions to cash. 
    
    [1] (https://dividendmapper.com/blog/transfer-isa-to-trading-212), 
    [2] (https://www.tiktok.com/discover/how-to-transfer-stocks-from-trading-212-to-trade-republic)

- Asset availability match: 

Selected ETFs must exist on Trade Republic. 

[1] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer), 
[2] (https://dividendmapper.com/blog/transfer-isa-to-trading-212)

## How to Initiate the Transfer

- Download the form: Get the document inside Trade Republic app.
	- Access via Wealth settings.
	- Tap Securities Transfer menu. 
    [1] (https://traderepublic.com/en-de/support)

- Submit details: Deliver the completed document to Trading 212.
	- Include your account ID. 
    
    [1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007081117-Can-I-move-my-Trading-212-ISA-to-another-provider), 
    [2] (https://traderepublic.com/en-de/support)

- Expect processing wait: 
The timeline usually takes under three weeks. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-sk/139-How-long-does-a-depot-transfer-from-Trade-Republic-to-a-third_party-depot-take)

## Community Experiences

One point of agreement among users on Reddit is that transfers between these two specific platforms 
often face significant administrative delays, with both customer support teams occasionally blaming 
each other for a standstill. Make sure all personal data matches perfectly on both accounts to avoid 
bottlenecks. 

[1] (https://www.reddit.com/r/trading212/comments/1cbtdv2/portfolio_transfer/)

# Trading 212 transfer ETF to other broker

[Transfer your portfolio from and to other brokers](https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer)  

[How To Transfer Trading 212 Portfolio to Interactive Brokers Smart Money with Kai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1sjGimAgJo)  

[Trading 212 Portfolio Transfers: A Beginners Step by Step Guide Dr. Jubair's Finance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oxAUNdwbV_c)

You can transfer whole-share ETF holdings out of Trading 212 to another broker without selling your positions, though specific rules and restrictions apply. [1] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer), [2] (https://curvo.eu/article/trading-212-review), [3] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/30772347994653-What-is-a-SIPP-transfer)

Key Rules & Requirements

• Whole shares only: You can only transfer complete/whole units of ETFs. [1] (https://curvo.eu/article/trading-212-review)
• Fractional shares: Fractional shares cannot be transferred in-specie (as-is); if you leave or close parts of your holding, fractional shares must be liquidated into cash. [1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/30772347994653-What-is-a-SIPP-transfer), [2] (https://curvo.eu/article/trading-212-review)
• Trading 212 Fees: Trading 212 does not charge an outgoing transfer fee, though your new receiving broker may charge their own fees. [1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/16105401083805-What-is-a-portfolio-transfer), [2] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer)
• Account/Region limitations: Outgoing and incoming portfolio transfers depend on your account type (Invest or ISA) and the specific Trading 212 entity you are registered with (note: some restrictions apply by region, such as Germany for Trading 212 EU GmbH). [1] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer), [2] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/16105401083805-What-is-a-portfolio-transfer)

How to Initiate an Outgoing Transfer

1. Contact your new broker: Generally, outgoing portfolio transfers are initiated from the receiving broker's end. You will need to fill out their transfer authority or portfolio transfer request form using your Trading 212 account details. [1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007081117-Can-I-move-my-Trading-212-ISA-to-another-provider), [2] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/30772347994653-What-is-a-SIPP-transfer)
2. Specify assets: Indicate that you want an in-specie (or whole-share) transfer of your eligible ETF positions rather than a cash liquidation (unless you prefer to sell and move cash). [1] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer), [2] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007081117-Can-I-move-my-Trading-212-ISA-to-another-provider)
3. Timeline: Outgoing stock and ETF portfolio transfers can take anywhere from a few weeks to over a month depending on the brokers involved. Trading is typically frozen for the assets undergoing the transfer. [1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/30772347994653-What-is-a-SIPP-transfer)
• Check the official Trading 212 Portfolio Transfer Help Centre for up-to-date eligibility and support details. [1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/16105401083805-What-is-a-portfolio-transfer)

# Are PAC available on all ETF on Trade Republic?

No, a PAC (Piano di Accumulo del Capitale, or savings plan) is not available on every 
single ETF on Trade Republic. 

[1] (https://extraetf.com/it/pac-etf/offerta-pac-etf-trade-republic)

- Available Selection: 
Trade Republic offers over 2,000 to 2,500 ETFs eligible for a PAC 
(many of which have €0 execution fees), out of the total tradeable instruments 
on the platform. 

[1] (https://www.finanzapp.io/blog-ita/pac-etf-250-euro-al-mese), 
[2] (https://www.finanzapp.io/blog-ita/migliori-etf-pac-italia-2026), 
[3] (https://extraetf.com/it/pac-etf/offerta-pac-etf-trade-republic)

- Not Universal: 
While a massive range of popular equity, bond, commodity, and real estate ETFs 
support automated savings plans, niche or newly listed ETFs might only be available 
for single-order trading or not supported for periodic PAC investments.

You can check specific ETF eligibility directly inside the app under the `Security/Investment` 
tab or via the justETF Trade Republic Directory. 

[1] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/trade-republic-piano-di-accumulo.html)

# Is there a limit to the number of time I can stop or change the amount of a PAC on Trade Republic?

No, there is no limit to the number of times you can stop, pause, or change the amount of a savings plan 
(PAC - Piano di Accumulo) on Trade Republic. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/760-Cosa-sono-i-piani-di-accumulo)

You retain full flexibility over your investments, allowing you to modify your strategy as your 
financial situation changes. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/760-What-are-savings-plans), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

## Key Rules & Flexibility Rules

- Completely free updates: 
Adjusting the amount, frequency, or asset involves zero fees. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/760-Cosa-sono-i-piani-di-accumulo)

- Unlimited cancellations: 
Delete and restart a plan whenever you want. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

- No lock-in periods: 
Your accumulated fractional shares remain safely in your custody even if you stop the plan. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/760-What-are-savings-plans), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo)

- Automatic insufficient fund handling: 
If your cash balance is empty on execution day, Trade Republic simply skips that month's deposit 
without penalizing or charging you. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/910-Cosa-succede-se-nel-conto-di-transito-non-ci-sono-fondi-sufficienti-per-eseguire-il-piano-di-investimento)

## How to Make Changes in the App

1. Open the app and navigate to Wealth (or Investments). 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/890-Quando-viene-eseguito-il-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/en-si/760-What-are-savings-plans)

2. Tap Orders & Insights and select your active Savings Plan. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/890-Quando-viene-eseguito-il-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

3. Select the specific asset plan you want to modify. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo)

4. Tap Change to adjust the execution amount/frequency, or tap Delete to stop it entirely. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/761-Come-posso-interrompere-un-piano-di-accumulo), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

# How do pac work on trade republic?

[How to Set Up an AUTOMATIC FREE Savings Plan on TRADE REPUBLIC 📈 (ETFs, STOCKS, or CRYPTO) simodefa](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_F5sqiB_2hE)  

A PAC (Piano di Accumulo del Capitale, or savings plan) on Trade Republic 
allows you to automatically invest a fixed amount of money at regular intervals 
into ETFs, stocks, or cryptocurrencies. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

## How It Works

- Choose the Asset: 
Select the ETF, stock, or crypto asset you want to invest in using the app 
(searching by ISIN is recommended to pick the exact instrument). 

[1] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/trade-republic-piano-di-accumulo.html), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

- Set the Amount & Frequency: 
Decide how much you want to invest (even small amounts are supported) and choose a 
frequency such as weekly, monthly, bi-monthly, or quarterly. 

[1] (https://www.finanzapp.io/blog-ita/pac-etf-250-euro-al-mese), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)

- Execution Date & Funding: 
Pick your preferred execution date (e.g., start or middle of the month). 
The money is automatically debited from your Trade Republic cash account (or via direct debit if set up). 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/890-Quando-viene-eseguito-il-piano-di-risparmio), 
[2] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio), 
[3] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/trade-republic-piano-di-accumulo.html)

- Fractional Shares: 
If your recurring investment amount does not equal the exact price of a full share, 
Trade Republic automatically purchases fractional shares for you. 

[1] (https://www.tiktok.com/@futurascf/video/7675305547196812566)

- Free Modification or Cancellation: 
You can pause, edit the amount/frequency, or cancel the savings plan for free at any time 
directly inside the app. 

[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)
You can set up a plan via the Trade Republic Support Guide. 
[1] (https://support.traderepublic.com/it-it/911-Come-posso-creare-un-piano-di-risparmio)
