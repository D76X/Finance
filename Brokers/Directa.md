## Can I transfer my ETF from Trading 212 to DIRECTA in Italy?

Yes, you can transfer your ETF portfolio out of Trading 212 to another broker, 
and Directa (an Italian broker) does allow incoming securities transfers. However, 
there are important practical and tax details to keep in mind: 

[1] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer), 
[2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1sjGimAgJo&t=44), 
[3] (https://brokerchooser.com/broker-reviews/directa-review/directa-alternatives-italy)

## How the Transfer Works

- Trading 212's side: 
Trading 212 supports outgoing portfolio transfers (in-specie) for whole shares 
(fractional shares cannot be transferred). Trading 212 does not charge an outgoing transfer fee. 
You initiate or coordinate this via the app/platform under portfolio transfers, and the receiving 
broker (Directa) must officially request it. 

[1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/16105401083805-What-is-a-portfolio-transfer), 
[2] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007081117-Can-I-move-my-Trading-212-ISA-to-another-provider), 
[3] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1sjGimAgJo&t=44), 
[4] (https://brokerchooser.com/broker-reviews/directa-review/directa-alternatives-italy)

- Directa's side: 
Directa accepts incoming transfers of securities (trasferimento titoli), though you must check with 
Directa's support whether they charge any per-position/per-line fees or if the specific ETFs you hold 
are supported on their platform. 
[1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1sjGimAgJo&t=44), 
[2] (https://brokerchooser.com/broker-reviews/directa-review/directa-alternatives-italy)

- Tax Regime Change: 
Moving from Trading 212 (which typically operates under the regime dichiarativo for Italian tax residents, 
where you or your accountant report capital gains manually) to Directa—which offers the regime amministrato 
(where the broker acts as a withholding agent and pays taxes on your behalf)—means your tax handling will shift. 
If moving to Directa's regime amministrato, Directa will need the historical purchase price (load/cost basis) 
of your ETFs to calculate future capital gains correctly, or you may face administrative hurdles. 

[1] (https://community.trading212.com/t/add-italian-stock-and-etf/47490)

- Timeline: 
Outgoing and incoming in-specie transfers can take anywhere from a couple of weeks up to a month, during 
which your shares will be locked and cannot be traded. 

[1] (https://www.trading212.com/portfolio-transfer), 
[2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q1sjGimAgJo&t=44)

> Tip: 
Because international or cross-platform in-specie transfers can sometimes be slow or prone to 
administrative friction with cost-basis tracking, it is recommended to contact Directa customer support first to confirm their current procedure and any paperwork required for an incoming transfer from Trading 212.
