# Scalable Capital regime amministrato

Scalable Capital offers the regime amministrato (administered tax regime) 
in Italy following the launch of its local branch and Italian IBAN. 

[1] (https://it.scalable.capital/en/newsroom/italy-bank-branch-raise), 
[2] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/whats-the-difference-between-regime-amministrato-and-re-5020d8f3)

## How It Works

- Withholding Agent (Sostituto d'Imposta): 
Scalable Capital calculates, withholds, and pays your capital gains taxes and related fees directly. 

[1] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/whats-the-difference-between-regime-amministrato-and-re-5020d8f3), 
[2] (https://it.scalable.capital/en/newsroom/italy-bank-branch-raise)

- No Tax Reporting: 
You do not need to manually report your Scalable account investments in your annual 
Italian tax return (dichiarazione dei redditi) for the periods covered under this regime. 

[1] (https://it.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch), 
[2] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/whats-the-difference-between-regime-amministrato-and-re-5020d8f3)

- Automatic Loss Offsetting: 
Scalable automatically checks your loss pot and offsets capital losses against eligible 
gains when you sell assets, carrying forward losses for up to 4 years. Note that certain 
income types like dividends or UCITS ETF gains (redditi di capitale) cannot be reduced 
by capital losses. 

[1] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/how-are-my-losses-offset-under-regime-amministrato-2d5a5137)

## Choosing & Switching Regimes

- Optional Choice: 
You can choose between Regime Amministrato and Regime Dichiarativo (where you handle the tax reporting yourself). 

[1] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/do-i-have-to-choose-regime-amministrato-c0d7116b), 
[2] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/scalable-regime-amministrato-italia.html)

- Changing Your Mind: 
You can switch your tax regime at any time in your account under `Profile > Tax information > Tax regime`. 

[1] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/whats-the-difference-between-regime-amministrato-and-re-5020d8f3)

- Timeline for Changes: 
A change requested during the year takes effect starting 1 January of the following year. 

[1] (https://www.justetf.com/it/academy/scalable-regime-amministrato-italia.html), 
[2] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/whats-the-difference-between-regime-amministrato-and-re-5020d8f3)

- Past Tax Obligations: 
If you switch or activate the regime mid-year, any prior period under the regime dichiarativo 
remains your personal responsibility to declare. 

[1] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/do-i-still-need-to-file-my-taxes-for-2026-even-if-i-cho-ee21cb3d), 
[2] (https://it.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch)

More details and account settings are available directly via the Scalable Capital 
Italian Branch Info or the Scalable Capital Help Center. 

[1] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch-0e68dfd5/taxes-7d44a39c/whats-the-difference-between-regime-amministrato-and-re-5020d8f3), 
[2] (https://it.scalable.capital/en/italian-branch)

# Can I transfer my ETF from Trading 212 to Scalable Capital in Italy?

No, you generally cannot do an in-specie (direct share/ETF) transfer of your portfolio 
from Trading 212 to Scalable Capital in Italy. 

[1] (https://community.trading212.com/t/portfolio-transfer-to-trading-212/29363), 
[2] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/securities-transfer-10e8cd05/can-i-transfer-my-securities-account-from-a-foreign-bank-325a6432)

## Key Reasons:

- Scalable Capital's Restrictions: 
Scalable Capital currently only accepts incoming portfolio/securities transfers from 
specific accounts (primarily with a German IBAN/BIC, with very limited exceptions like 
Trade Republic and Degiro) and does not accept general securities transfers from foreign/other 
neobroker accounts. 

[1] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/securities-transfer-10e8cd05/can-i-transfer-my-securities-account-from-a-foreign-bank-325a6432)

- Trading 212 Limitations: 

While Trading 212 supports outgoing portfolio transfers in certain regions, moving assets 
in-specie (without selling) to a platform that doesn't ingest them is not supported. 

[1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360007081117-Can-I-move-my-Trading-212-ISA-to-another-provider), 
[2] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/securities-transfer-10e8cd05/can-i-transfer-my-securities-account-from-a-foreign-bank-325a6432), 
[3] (https://community.trading212.com/t/portfolio-transfer-to-trading-212/29363)

## Alternative Option:

If you still want to move your investments from Trading 212 to Scalable Capital in Italy, you have to:

1. Sell your ETFs on Trading 212.
2. Withdraw the cash to your bank account.
3. Transfer the cash to Scalable Capital via SEPA transfer.
4. Re-buy the ETFs on Scalable Capital. 

[1] (https://de.scalable.capital/en/newsroom/en-scalable-italy), 
[2] (https://help.scalable.capital/en/overnight-account-fdf4fe44/how-long-do-deposits-and-withdrawals-take-cfe47e7b), 
[3] (https://de.scalable.capital/en/product-news/deposits-withdrawals)

(Note: Selling your assets may trigger a taxable event or capital gains realization depending on your tax jurisdiction and tax regime in Italy).
