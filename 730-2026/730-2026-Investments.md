# 730

## Sections for monitoring and taxes on investments

### Section W for monitoring and taxes on investments

The 730/2025 model (2024 income) introduces the Section W for monitoring and taxes on investments 
and financial/crypto assets abroad, replacing the old Section RW. 

### Section T

For capital gains and returns, the new Section T (capital gains/losses, formerly RT) 
and Section M (foreign capital income, formerly RM) are used, centralizing investment 
management in the 730 form.

### Main News and 730/2025 Forms for Investments:

- Section W (Monitoring and Assets): 
Mandatory for residents with assets abroad (accounts, shares, cryptocurrencies). 
Calculates IVIE (real estate) and IVAFE (financial products).

- Section T (Capital Gains/Losses): 
Dedicated to foreign financial capital gains and crypto-assets (26% or 20% in specific cases).

- Section M (Foreign Income): 
Reserved for dividends and coupons on foreign investments.

- Table D (Other income): 
For capital income subject to IRPEF.

### Key Points:

- Crypto-assets: 
These should be monitored in Table W and capital gains taxed in Table T.

- Deadline: 
Tax payments are due via F24 by June 30, 2025 (or July 30 with an additional 0.4% tax).

- Foreign accounts: 
Even if closed during the year, they must be declared.

- Investments in Italy: 
If managed by Italian intermediaries (managed regime), capital gains do not appear 
in these tables since taxation is handled by the intermediary.
