# CAN I MOVE AN ETF FROM A PIE TO ANOTHER with Trading 212?

Yes, you can move an ETF from one Pie to another on Trading 212 without selling it. 

[1] (https://community.trading212.com/t/pies-import-export-investments/17368), 
[2] (https://community.trading212.com/t/change-my-stock-isa-etf-stock-and-pie/88714/2)

How to Move an ETF Between Pies

1. Export the ETF from the first Pie:
	• Open your source Pie.
	• Tap the three dots (menu) in the top-right corner.
	• Select Export investments (or export the specific shares) to move the ETF out of the Pie and into your main/general investments account. 
    
    [1] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/30674563143581-Export-Import-Shares-into-Pies), 
    [2] (https://helpcentre.trading212.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014191598-Why-is-the-Sell-button-missing-on-my-Invest-ISA-position), 
    [3] (https://community.trading212.com/t/change-my-stock-isa-etf-stock-and-pie/88714/2)

2. Import the ETF into the second Pie:
	• Open your target Pie.
	• Go to Holdings and find the Import option (or tap the three dots / edit menu depending on your app version).
	• Select the ETF from your general portfolio to import it into the new Pie.
	• Adjust the target allocation weights of the new Pie so they total 100% and confirm.
